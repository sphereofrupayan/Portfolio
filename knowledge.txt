## MAILMATE

MailMate is a productivity-focused email workflow application developed during the Code2Create hackathon.

The project focuses on converting information from email conversations into structured and actionable workflow information.

Problem Statement:

Important tasks, deadlines, blockers, dependencies and other actionable information are often buried inside lengthy email conversations.

Users may need to manually read through multiple emails to understand:

* What needs to be done
* Who needs to do it
* What the deadline is
* Which tasks depend on other tasks
* Which issues are blocking progress
* What actions should be taken next

MailMate was designed to make this process more organized and efficient.

Core Concept:

MailMate helps transform unstructured email communication into actionable workflow information.

The system can focus on identifying:

* Tasks
* Action items
* Deadlines
* Dependencies
* Blockers
* Important information
* Follow-up actions

Key Features:

* Email workflow management
* Email analysis
* Action-item identification
* Task extraction
* Deadline identification
* Dependency tracking
* Blocker identification
* Task organization
* Workflow management
* Reminder-oriented workflow
* Calendar-oriented actions
* Centralized productivity interface

Project Goal:

The goal of MailMate is to reduce the amount of manual effort required to understand and organize important information contained in emails.

Instead of treating an inbox only as a collection of messages, MailMate focuses on turning email communication into actionable work.
